## Assumptions

- 6 overlapping 8-hour shifts per day, starting every 4 hours.
- Each shift has fixed positions (e.g. 1 technician, 1 operator) with required
  qualifications.
- At least one supervisor must be on the floor at every moment, across
  overlapping shifts.
- Employees are assigned from a shared pool, not fixed crews.
- One set of rules for the whole site, set in CONFIG: 12-hour minimum rest,
  a max-hours cap per roster period, weekend days and public holidays.
  Country-specific labour rules are out of scope.
- Leave is a time period; any shift overlapping it is blocked.
- A flexi day is a floating day from a yearly allowance, approved first come,
  first served, only if the roster can still be covered.
- Fairness covers total hours plus nights, weekends and holidays.
- If a roster is impossible, gaps are reported rather than rules broken.
